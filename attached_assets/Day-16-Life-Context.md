## Question 1

**In two or three sentences — where did you come from and what were you doing before your current work?**

I grew up in a financially difficult environment with limited opportunities and a strong sense of responsibility toward my family. Before my current work, I followed a more conventional employment path, but after losing or leaving that job I chose to focus on AI-assisted software, automation, and trading-strategy research. I am not formally trained as a professional programmer, quantitative researcher, or trader; most of my knowledge has been built through practical projects, experimentation, and working closely with AI tools.

## Question 2

**What are the two or three experiences that most shaped how you operate today?**

1. Financial pressure and responsibility toward my parents taught me that money represents security and independence, but also that urgency can make weak opportunities feel necessary.
2. Losing my job and changing direction taught me that stability matters, but relying on one employer should not be the only foundation of my future.
3. Trading losses and months spent on overcomplicated, unfinished projects taught me that activity is not progress, impressive numbers are not proof, and stopping rules should be defined before emotional investment grows.

## Question 3

**What are your core operating principles?**

- Evidence before confidence.
- Completion and validation before expansion.
- Move fast when failure is reversible; slow down when consequences are difficult to undo.
- Essential and family money is not risk capital.
- Integrity over profit.
- AI autonomy must be earned inside strict limits.
- Focus on one meaningful priority at a time.
- Reliability is more valuable than impressiveness.

## Question 4

**Where do you tend to make bad calls?**

My judgment becomes weaker when financial pressure creates urgency, when a new tool or feature makes expansion feel like progress, or when I have already invested enough time that stopping feels wasteful. I can also over-monitor uncertain financial risks and give too much authority to confident technical or trading explanations in areas where my formal knowledge is limited.

## Question 5

**What else should an agent working closely with you know?**

Supporting my parents is non-negotiable and one of the strongest reasons behind my goals. I want a focused solo business earning more than $10,000 per month in recurring revenue, with reliable automation and eventually four to six focused work hours per day. I prefer direct recommendations with reasoning, the main downside, the best alternative, and the next action. I do not have a fixed team of professional advisers, so the agent should identify when specialist legal, tax, cybersecurity, architecture, or quantitative advice is needed.

## Day 5 — Who Are You? (Personality and Communication Preferences)

You are helping me complete Day 5 of the Zero One Systems curriculum.

When we're done, I will have a short personality profile I can use as input when writing my soul.md.

---

Ask me the following questions one at a time. Wait for my full answer before moving to the next.

1. What was your 16Personalities result? Give me your four-letter type (e.g. INTJ, ENFP) and paste any sub-trait scores or details from your result if you have them.

2. Which parts of the description felt most accurate? Be specific — which traits or tendencies do you genuinely recognise in yourself?

3. Which parts felt least accurate or didn't apply?

4. How do you like information delivered to you? Think about how your best colleagues or managers have communicated with you — what did they get right?

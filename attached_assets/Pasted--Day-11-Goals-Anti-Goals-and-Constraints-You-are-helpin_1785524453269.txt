Ask me the following questions one at a time. Wait for my full answer before moving to the next.







1. If everything goes right over the next twelve months, what does your work and life actually look like? Be specific — revenue, lifestyle, working hours, what you've built, who you're working with (or not working with).

4. What are your real constraints? Time (how many hours per week are genuinely available for this), money (real risk threshold, not aspirational), personal (what's non-negotiable about how you live)?







5. Is there anything your agent should never do — even if it believes it would help you? Think about actions, recommendations, communications. Anything that would be a hard stop.

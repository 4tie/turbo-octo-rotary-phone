# Business Context

## What I'm Building

**AI Strategy Research Platform:** My primary business direction is a focused AI-assisted platform for discovering, testing, improving, validating, and rejecting Freqtrade trading strategies under realistic conditions. The product is intended to combine structured strategy research, backtesting, optimisation, out-of-sample testing, walk-forward analysis, robustness checks, multi-pair validation, and clear evidence-based promotion or rejection rules. The intended business model is recurring revenue through subscriptions, paid access, or premium automation features, but it is not yet a proven revenue-generating business and should not be described as one.

**Controlled AI Trading System:** I am also exploring a separate system that can analyse markets, explain possible entries and exits, calculate risk, and eventually assist with or manage trades under strict limits. This is currently a research and development project, not a dependable income source. It must remain separate from essential money and from claims that it can trade profitably until it has passed strong validation and controlled live testing.

## Current Activities

- Completing and stabilising the core strategy-research platform instead of continuing to expand its scope.
- Connecting the full research pipeline so strategies move through data checks, baseline testing, optimisation, out-of-sample validation, robustness testing, portfolio evaluation, and export with clear status and evidence.
- Improving the AI agent so it understands the project architecture, evaluation rules, current decisions, runtime environment, and previous experiment history rather than giving generic trading or software advice.
- Verifying that implemented features work through real tests and distinguishing clearly between planned, implemented, tested, and production-ready work.
- Reducing duplicated systems, unnecessary agents, branches, features, and redesigns that make the project harder to finish.

## Constraints

- **Team:** I am working primarily alone and rely heavily on AI coding assistants. I do not have a permanent engineering, quantitative research, legal, tax, security, or product team.
- **Skills:** I have practical familiarity with AI-assisted software projects, Freqtrade, trading systems, backtesting, and automation, but I am not a professional software architect, quantitative researcher, statistician, or trader. Important assumptions and specialist decisions must be explained and verified.
- **Capital:** My budget is limited. I prefer local, free, or low-cost tools, and I cannot justify large fixed costs, heavy cloud spending, or experiments that threaten living expenses or family support.
- **Time:** More than 40 hours per week are currently available, but focus is the real constraint. Switching projects, researching new tools, redesigning, and adding features before completion reduce progress.
- **Risk:** Essential savings, household money, and money needed to support my parents are not risk capital. Live trading must remain limited, controlled, and secondary to building the business.
- **Commercial experience:** The product does not yet have proven recurring revenue, a validated customer base, or a confirmed repeatable acquisition channel.

## Current Goals

- **Immediate:** Finish and stabilise one usable end-to-end version of the strategy-research platform, with the core pipeline working and tested, before adding major new features or projects.
- **Near term:** Validate that the platform solves a real problem for users, define a clear initial customer, and release a version that real users can try rather than keeping it as an internal prototype.
- **Within the next year:** Build a profitable solo business generating more than $10,000 per month in consistent recurring revenue from multiple customers, not from a single trading result.
- **Operational:** Make the platform increasingly automated and reliable so it can eventually operate with approximately four to six focused hours of my work per day rather than requiring permanent overwork.
- **Research quality:** Ensure strategies are accepted only when they show positive expectancy and stability after realistic fees and slippage, sufficient trades, out-of-sample testing, walk-forward analysis, robustness testing, and multi-market or multi-pair validation.
- **Financial safety:** Build a financial buffer that allows me to support my parents consistently and make business decisions without panic or dependence on high-risk trading.

## State of Play

The strongest part of the business is the clarity of the problem and the amount of work already done on the research workflow, validation philosophy, risk rules, user interface, optimiser, and agent-assisted operation. The project has a meaningful technical foundation and a clear principle: evidence before confidence.

The main weakness is completion and commercial validation. The platform has repeatedly grown in complexity through new features, agents, workflows, and redesigns, while the core product still needs to be finished, simplified, tested end to end, and placed in front of real users. There is not yet dependable recurring revenue, a proven customer segment, or evidence that the trading system itself can safely produce reliable profits. The current priority is therefore to finish, verify, release, and learn — not expand.
